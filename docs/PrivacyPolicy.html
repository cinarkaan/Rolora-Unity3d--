Data Collection

This application uses Google Play Games Services (GPGS) to provide optional features such as cloud saves and achievements. When you sign in with GPGS, the application may access your Google Play Games display name and avatar for use in local multiplayer sessions. Your game progress and inventory data, including PlayerData and MapData, may be stored using Google Play Saved Games to synchronize your progress across devices.

Data Usage

Your display name and avatar are used to identify and display players during local multiplayer sessions. Game progress and inventory data are used to provide game saves, synchronize your progress across devices, and maintain game functionality. Achievement-related information is used to provide and maintain achievements through Google Play Games Services.

Google Play Games Services

Google Play Games Services is optional. If you are unable to connect to Google Play Games Services or choose not to sign in, you can continue playing the game offline. Cloud saves, achievements, and GPGS profile features are available when you are signed in to Google Play Games Services.

Local Wi-fi Multiplayer

During local wi-fi multiplayer sessions, your display name and avatar may be transmitted to and displayed to other players participating in the same local wi-fi multiplayer session. This information is used solely to identify players within the game. For Android-17(API 37) or higher versions 'ACCESS_LOCAL_NETWORK' permission is to necessary for local-wifi multiplayer.

In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are processed securely through Google Play. The application does not collect or store your payment card number, CVV, bank account information, or other payment credentials. Purchase processing is handled by Google Play according to its own terms and privacy policies.

Data Sharing

I do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising or other independent purposes. We do not operate external custom servers for storing your game data. Data stored through Google Play Games Services is managed through Google Play infrastructure. Information required for local wi-fi multiplayer, such as your display name and avatar, may be shared directly with other players participating in the same local wi-fi multiplayer session.

Security

Data transmitted between the application and Google Play services is protected using encryption in transit. Local game data is stored on your device.

User Rights & Data Deletion

You can reset or delete your local and cloud game progress, including collected items and inventory, by selecting the "New Game" option and confirming the deletion when prompted. The game will reset the relevant local and cloud save data. You may also contact us to request assistance with deletion of cloud-stored game data or account-linked information.

Account Unlinking & Access Revocation

You can manage, revoke, or disconnect the application’s access to your Google Play Games profile at any time. You can do this directly through the Google Play Games app settings or via your Google Account Permissions (Google Security / Connected Apps center).

Contact
